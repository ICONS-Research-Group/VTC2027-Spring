Workshop Scope

Next-generation Internet of Things (IoT) and Internet of Vehicles (IoV) systems are becoming increasingly autonomous, interconnected, and intelligence-driven. Their communication, computing, and control infrastructures must sustain high mobility, dense connectivity, heterogeneous services, and stringent latency, reliability, and safety requirements. Digital twins (DTs) offer continuously updated virtual representations of vehicles, networks, and transportation environments, while pervasive artificial intelligence (AI) distributes learning, inference, prediction, and decision-making across vehicles, roadside units, edge servers, and cloud platforms. Together, they can form a bidirectional physical-virtual intelligence loop: DTs support data-driven modeling, experimentation, and optimization, while AI improves state estimation, prediction, adaptation, and closed-loop control. Key barriers remain. Rapid mobility, time-varying channels, incomplete observations, and evolving topologies can delay updates, desynchronize twins, and widen the physical-virtual domain gap. At the same time, large-scale twin maintenance and distributed AI increase communication and computing load, which can reduce information freshness and decision quality.

This half-day workshop will convene researchers and practitioners to advance scalable, trustworthy, and resource-aware DT-AI systems for next-generation IoT/IoV. Topics include adaptive synchronization, domain adaptation, edge-cloud collaboration, task partitioning, cooperative processing, computation offloading, distributed inference, joint communication-computation orchestration, and validation through prototypes, testbeds, and field trials.

  • •Security, privacy, and trust in DT-AI-enabled IoT/IoV ecosystems
  • •Interoperability and standardization of DT and AI technologies for IoT/IoV and 6G
  • •Big data management and real-time analytics for large-scale digital twins
  • •DT-AI-enabled applications in intelligent transportation and smart infrastructure

Applications & Experimentation

  • •UAV/drone-assisted IoT/IoV sensing and monitoring using DT and AI
  • •Experimental platforms, testbeds, and field trials for DT-AI-enabled IoT/IoV
  • •Cross-domain innovations enabled by DT-AI synergy in smart mobility and beyond

Call for Papers

This workshop invites high-quality original research contributions that explore theories, architectures, algorithms, and experimental systems for synergizing digital twins and pervasive intelligence in next-generation IoT/IoV. Both theoretical advances and practical insights from testbeds, prototypes, and field trials are encouraged.
